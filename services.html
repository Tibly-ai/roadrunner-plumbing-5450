Residential Repairs

Leaky faucets, running toilets, slab leaks, clogged drains, slow-filling tubs. If it's part of the plumbing in your house, we've probably fixed one just like it in Yucca Valley or Twentynine Palms this month. We also handle full or partial repiping when a place has old galvanized or corroded lines that keep failing.

  • •Leak detection and repair, indoor and outdoor
  • •Drain clearing and clog removal
  • •Fixture repair and replacement (faucets, toilets, valves)
  • •Full and partial home repiping
02

Water Heater Repair & Installation

Desert water is hard on tanks. We repair and replace both standard tank units and tankless systems, and we'll tell you straight which one makes sense for your house and your water use. No cold showers longer than they have to be.

  • •Tank water heater repair and replacement
  • •Tankless water heater installation
  • •Sediment flushes and hard water fixes
  • •Same-week installs on most units
